# SAT Nexus — Ideas Awaiting Approval

These are intentionally **not implemented**. They are candidates for the owner to approve, reject, or revise before development.

1. **Spaced-repetition study plans** — schedule vocabulary, formulas, and missed skills based on recall strength.
2. **Calendar and reminders** — test-date countdown, weekly goals, and opt-in desktop notifications.
3. **Cloud sync and accounts** — encrypted progress sync between desktop and web, with local-only mode remaining available.
4. **Import/export and backups** — portable JSON/CSV backup for progress, notes, collections, and settings.
5. **Official score calibration tables** — versioned practice-test conversion data if College Board publishes enough information to support it.
6. **Full-length test resume** — save an in-progress timed test and safely resume after closing the app.
7. **Teacher/tutor view** — share selected reports or assignments without exposing unrelated personal study data.
8. **Study-plan generator** — build a weekly plan from a target score, test date, available days, and weak domains.
9. **Deeper vocabulary tools** — pronunciation audio, roots, synonym families, and user-created vocabulary decks.
10. **Worked-example lessons** — step-by-step concept lessons before practice, with optional hints rather than immediate solutions.
11. **Question annotations** — highlighting, scratch notes, and bookmarks attached to exact passage ranges.
12. **Offline Tauri updater and signed releases** — packaged desktop updates, migration backups, and rollback support.
13. **Calculator/scratchpad upgrades** — graph state saving, geometry sketching, and reusable scratch work.
14. **Accessibility audit mode** — contrast reports, keyboard-only testing, dyslexia-friendly font option, and screen-reader walkthroughs.
15. **Parent/guardian controls** — optional goal summaries while keeping answers, notes, and detailed activity private by default.

No item in this document should be built until the owner explicitly approves it.
